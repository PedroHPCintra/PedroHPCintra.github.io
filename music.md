# Samba

Samba is a broad music genre with it's subgenres originated on different regions of the country. For instance the _samba de roda_ was originated in Bahia (northeast region of the country), while the _samba de bumbo_ had it's origin on the state of São Paulo (more to the southeast). Today, the most recongnized style of samba is the urban carioca samba, born from afro-brazilian communities at Rio de Janeiro. On the XIX century, approximately half of the population at Brazil's capital (which was Rio de Janeiro at that time) was composed of black people. One slavery was abolished in 1888, many black people from Bahia migrated to the capital seeking better life conditions. Heitor de Prazeres, a pioneer on samba who participated on the foundation of the first samba schools, referred to Rio de Janeiro as "Little Africa". This migration and the marginalization of the black population led to the foundation of the first candomblé terreiros, where the _sambda de roda_ from Bahia would acquire characteristics from the carioca (carioca refers to those born at the city of Rio de Janeiro) culture and originate the urban carioca samba.

For those more interested the [wikipédia page about samba](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samba) in portuguese is very detailed on the history of the genre and has some good references, like the book **Dicionário da História Social do Samba**.
